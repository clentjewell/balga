Citations Playbook — Balga Designs

How to work this playbook

This is a hand-worked checklist, not an automated submitter. Sit down with the Canonical Listing Block below open in one window and each directory in another, then copy the facts across verbatim.

  • Work top-down. Do the High priority targets first, starting with Google Business Profile — it is the single most valuable listing and everything else reinforces it.
  • Keep the NAP byte-identical everywhere. Same name, same phone format, same email, same website spelling on every site. Inconsistent Name / Address / Phone (NAP) data is what confuses search engines and weakens citations. Copy and paste — never retype.
  • Record every live listing URL. As each listing goes live, paste its public URL into the URL column and set Status to Live. You will need those URLs later to re-verify the business or fix details if the NAP ever changes.
  • One fact source. If a detail changes (phone, hours, a new service), update the Canonical Listing Block first, then push the change out to every recorded listing URL.

Long description (~150 words)

Balga Designs is a sustainable landscape and native garden design studio based in Lennox Head, working across the Northern Rivers of New South Wales through to the Southern Gold Coast. We design naturalistic, native-led gardens that are rooted in Country — spaces where beauty and the health of the local ecosystem grow together. Our ecological, regenerative approach favours indigenous planting, healthy soil and habitat that supports pollinators, birds and the wider landscape.

From a full landscape design to a garden facelift, decorative pot styling, integrated pest management and horticulture or weed-control consultation, we help homeowners create gardens that feel settled in place and get better with age. As a service-area studio we come to you across Lennox Head, Ballina, Byron Bay and beyond. Warm, considered and nature-led, Balga Designs makes gardens that give back to the country they sit in.
