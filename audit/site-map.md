## Global chrome

- **Header** (all pages): logo (white, with tagline) top-left → horizontal nav
  `Home · About · Services · Projects · Pricing · Blog` → `Contact Us` pill
  button top-right. Nav collapses to a hamburger / slide-in menu below the
  1024px breakpoint. Header overlays the hero (transparent) on every page.
- **Footer** (all pages): brand-green `#5A674E` background, 3 columns — brand +
  Acknowledgement of Country, "Get In Touch" contact list, "Quicklinks" and
  "Support" link columns, Facebook + Instagram icons, copyright + "Develop by
  Jewell Projects".
